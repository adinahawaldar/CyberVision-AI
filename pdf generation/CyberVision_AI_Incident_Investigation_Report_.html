13. RECOMMENDED FOLLOW-UP ACTIONS

The following are general operational actions. They do not imply any finding or conclusion.

  • Review the complete CCTV recording surrounding the detected event.
  • Verify the configured restricted-area boundary and detection rule settings.
  • Correlate the event with access-control logs, if available.
  • Preserve the original CCTV recording and export the corresponding evidence files.
  • Review related camera feeds covering adjacent areas.
  • Independently verify all AI-generated detections and annotations.
  • Record investigator observations in the Investigation Notes section.
